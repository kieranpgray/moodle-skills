# PRD writing standard

Every sentence in a PRD is tested against this page before it goes in.

The test for every word: would you say it to a teacher across a desk? If not, swap it.

---

## Two tests for every sentence

1. **Who is doing or seeing something?** If no person or role appears, rewrite.
2. **Could you photograph it?** "Posts become long lines on a large screen" passes. "A better reading experience" fails.

A word list alone gets gamed ("seamless" becomes "smooth"). The tests come first; the lists back them up.

---

## Banned words and their swaps

"Initiative", "ensure" and "modern" are allowed.

**Hype adjectives**

| Ban | Say instead |
|---|---|
| seamless | what survives: "Nobody loses their dashboard" |
| effortless | what they can now do: "reply without leaving the page" |
| robust, solid | what it withstands: "works on a poor connection" |
| transform | change, rebuild, replace |
| hub, space, ecosystem, journey (as metaphors) | name the actual page or person |
| golden thread, springboard, laser focused, and similar | cut |

**Consultant verbs**

| Ban | Say instead |
|---|---|
| leverage, utilise | use |
| facilitate | help, let |
| enable, empower (people) | let, so they can |
| unlock (as hype) | makes X possible, and name X |
| streamline, optimise | faster, fewer steps, with the number |
| drive (adoption, value) | say who does what |

**Nouns hiding a verb**

| Ban | Say instead |
|---|---|
| provides the ability to | lets teachers |
| implementation of, adoption of | when we build, when sites turn on |
| visibility into | can see |
| value proposition, competitive differentiator | why someone would pick Moodle |

---

## Patterns to cut

- "Not X. Y." contrasts used more than once per page.
- Triplets of adjectives ("modern, accessible, and effortless").
- A bold-italic "vision" line that repeats the Summary.
- Inline "(Confirmed: …)" and ⚠️ on every line. Keep labels for the claims everything else rests on; link the rest to their source inline.
- Hedge stacks: potentially, may help to, aims to.
- Labels and headlines that sell rather than describe ("Competitive value", "Ecosystem compatibility").

---

## What good sounds like

Each has a person, a concrete detail, and short words.

- "Open a forum and you step back into the old one."
- "It repeats My Courses."
- "Educators see none of their work. They aren't enrolled in the courses they build."
- "One university wrote its own staff-only block to work around it."
- "Each new block makes the page longer, not more useful."
- "Removing a block doesn't ask 'Are you sure?'; it offers Undo instead."
- "YUI gets no fixes or security patches."
- "I'm terrified of hitting the enable button and breaking everything." (user quote)
- "Forum is on 98% of registered sites, because every course gets an Announcements forum."
